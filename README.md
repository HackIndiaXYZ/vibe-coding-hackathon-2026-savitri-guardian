# Savitri Guardian

## Identity. Context. Coordination. Because Every Second Matters.

**No emergency should begin with anonymity, uncertainty, or isolation.**

Savitri Guardian is the first implementation of the broader **Savitri Trusted Coordination Platform**, focused on medical emergencies and the Golden Hour.

The platform helps the right people receive the right information at the right time so they can take the right action.

---

## The Problem

Medical emergencies are rarely just medical problems.

When someone becomes unconscious, injured, confused, lost, or unable to communicate, the people around them are often left with critical unanswered questions:

* Who is this person?
* Who should be contacted?
* What medical information is important?
* Which hospital is involved?
* How do family members stay informed?
* How do responders coordinate?

The information often exists.

The coordination does not.

> **The first emergency is informational.**

---

## The Solution

Savitri Guardian creates a trusted coordination layer connecting:

* Individuals
* Families
* Public Helpers
* EMTs
* Hospitals

through privacy-aware emergency identity, trust, context, and coordination workflows.

A simple QR scan can initiate an accountable emergency response workflow without requiring app installation or prior registration.

---

## Why Savitri Is Different

### Accountable Public Assistance

Anyone can help.

No app installation.

No registration.

No account creation.

However, emergency access is not anonymous. Access requires consent acknowledgement, OTP verification, emergency declaration, and creates an auditable trail.

---

### Family-Centric Coordination

The people who would care if something happened to you are often not nearby.

Savitri helps them find you when you cannot reach them yourself.

---

### Trust Before Access

Medical information should not be public.

Medical information should not be hidden when its absence can create harm.

Savitri balances privacy, accountability, and emergency access through progressive trust and minimum necessary disclosure.

---

### AI-Assisted Coordination

AI helps transform information into actionable context through:

* Incident summaries
* Voice note summaries
* Emergency handoff generation
* Context consolidation

AI assists.

Humans decide.

---

## Demo Flow

1. Patient logs into Savitri Guardian
2. Emergency QR is displayed
3. Judge scans the QR
4. Emergency is declared
5. Reporter verifies identity using OTP
6. Family receives updates
7. EMT accepts the incident
8. Hospital receives context
9. AI generates handoff summaries
10. Coordinated response is delivered

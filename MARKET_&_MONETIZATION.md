# Market & Monetization

## Building Trusted Coordination Infrastructure

Savitri Guardian is the first implementation of the broader Savitri Trusted Coordination Platform.

While the initial focus is medical emergencies, the underlying platform addresses a much larger challenge:

> How do we coordinate the right people, information, and actions during critical life events?

---

# The Market Opportunity

Modern life has created a growing coordination gap.

People increasingly:

* Live away from family
* Travel frequently
* Care for aging parents remotely
* Support vulnerable family members
* Depend on fragmented healthcare systems
* Rely on disconnected organizations during emergencies

The people who would care if something happened to you are often not nearby.

Savitri exists to reduce that gap.

---

# Adoption Strategy

Savitri follows a simple principle:

> Help should always be free.

Any individual can create a profile, generate an Emergency Identity, and receive emergency assistance without paying.

The goal is to remove barriers during moments when help may be urgently needed and drastically increase adoption.

While emergency assistance remains free, advanced coordination capabilities may be offered through premium plans and institutional deployments.

This allows the platform to remain accessible while supporting long-term sustainability.

---

# Network-Driven Growth

Trusted coordination becomes more valuable as more participants join the ecosystem.

Whenever a user adds an emergency contact, caregiver, family member, or representative, that individual receives an invitation to join Savitri.

This creates a natural network effect:

Individual
→ Emergency Contact
→ Family Member
→ Caregiver
→ Organization

As coordination networks grow, the value of the platform increases for everyone involved.

The objective is not simply to acquire users.

The objective is to connect people who may need to coordinate during critical life events.

---

# Primary Adoption Wedges

## Young Professionals Away From Family

A growing population lives far from their support networks.

If an emergency occurs, family members often struggle to understand:

* What happened
* Where they are
* Who is helping

This wedge provides strong emotional urgency and natural viral adoption.

---

## Dementia & Cognitive Conditions

Families caring for loved ones with dementia face persistent concerns regarding identification, discoverability, and coordination.

The platform's identity and representation capabilities align naturally with this need.

---

## Travel & Relocation Safety

Tourists, business travelers, pilgrims, students, and migrant workers often find themselves far from their support networks.

This creates opportunities for both direct adoption and institutional partnerships.
